## Full job description

Backend Developer (Node.js / TypeScript)

**working hours:** EST time zone / Remote  
**Experience:** 2–3 Years  
**Employment Type:** Full-time

About the Role

We're looking for a Backend Developer with strong experience in **Node.js, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL** to build scalable backend systems and integrations. You'll work on high-volume data pipelines, third-party API integrations, asynchronous processing, and backend services powering e-commerce analytics and automation.

If you enjoy solving complex backend challenges, care about data accuracy, and have experience building production-ready systems, we'd love to hear from you.

Key Responsibilities

- Design, build, and maintain scalable backend services using **Node.js** and **TypeScript**.
- Develop and optimize **PostgreSQL** databases, including schema design, migrations, and query performance tuning using **Prisma ORM**.
- Build and maintain integrations with third-party REST APIs, handling OAuth authentication, token refresh, pagination, retries, and rate limits.
- Design and maintain asynchronous, event-driven systems using job queues such as **Inngest, BullMQ, Temporal, or AWS SQS**.
- Build reliable queue-processing pipelines with idempotent handlers, retry strategies, dead-letter queues, and duplicate message handling.
- Investigate and resolve production issues related to data accuracy, aggregations, and timezone handling.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver reliable, maintainable, and scalable backend solutions.

Must-Have Skills

- Strong hands-on experience with **TypeScript** and **Node.js**.
- Experience with **PostgreSQL** and ORM tools (preferably **Prisma**).
- Production experience integrating third-party REST APIs.
- Experience building or maintaining asynchronous queue-based systems.
- Strong debugging and problem-solving skills with a focus on data correctness.
- 2–3 years of backend development experience in production environments.

Strongly Preferred

- Experience with **Amazon Selling Partner API (SP-API)** or **Amazon Ads API** _(highly preferred)_.
- Working knowledge of **AWS**, especially **SQS** and **IAM**.
- Experience with **Next.js App Router** and API routes.
- Familiarity with **Redis**, **Docker**, and **CI/CD** pipelines.

Nice to Have

- Experience integrating LLM APIs such as **OpenAI**, **Anthropic**, or **Vercel AI SDK**.
- Knowledge of e-commerce or advertising technology, including **Amazon campaigns**, **ACOS**, and keyword bidding strategies.

What We're Looking For

- A developer who takes ownership and writes clean, maintainable code.
- Strong analytical and debugging skills.
- Someone comfortable working with distributed systems and production-scale backend services.
- A team player with excellent communication and problem-solving abilities.

If this sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you. Apply with your updated resume and, if available, links to your GitHub or portfolio.
