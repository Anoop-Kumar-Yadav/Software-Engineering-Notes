## Full job description

About BeSpoke AI Stylist

BeSpoke AI Stylist is an AI-powered fashion technology company focused on delivering personalized styling experiences through artificial intelligence, data-driven insights, and innovative digital solutions. We are building a scalable platform that helps users discover their style, manage wardrobes, and receive intelligent fashion recommendations.

Position Overview

We are seeking a skilled Backend Developer to build, maintain, and optimize the core infrastructure and APIs that power our AI styling platform. The ideal candidate will have strong experience in backend development, database management, cloud services, and scalable system design.

Key Responsibilities

- Design, develop, test, and maintain backend services and APIs.
- Build scalable and secure RESTful and GraphQL APIs to support web and mobile applications.
- Collaborate with AI Engineers, Frontend Developers, and Product teams to integrate AI-driven features.
- Design and manage databases, ensuring performance, security, and reliability.
- Develop and maintain authentication, authorization, and user management systems.
- Optimize application performance, scalability, and availability.
- Implement cloud-based infrastructure and deployment pipelines.
- Monitor system health, troubleshoot issues, and perform root cause analysis.
- Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code.
- Participate in code reviews and contribute to technical architecture decisions.

- Understanding of authentication protocols (OAuth, JWT, SSO).
